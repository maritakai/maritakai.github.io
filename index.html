The work

Agency projects are team efforts. I was the main designer on these, working alongside other designers, copywriters and the client to reach the final result.

Hi, I'm Marita.

I'm a graphic designer based in Beirut, Lebanon, and presentations are at the heart of what I do. Over five years I've designed pitch decks, corporate and annual reports, training content and company presentations that turn complex ideas into clear, confident slides people actually want to read.

Alongside presentations, I work across brand identity, web and UI design, social media and illustration. I've worked in creative agencies with designers and content creators, at an e-learning company, and as a freelancer for clinics, cosmetics brands, interior studios, restaurants and tech companies.

I like work that is clear at first glance, bold in its choices and a little unexpected. I'm open to freelance projects, from a single pitch deck or company presentation to a full brand identity.
